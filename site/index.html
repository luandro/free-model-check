How a route earns its entry

Three claims about price, one about quality.

iZero priced, dimension by dimension

A zero for every billable field. A missing pricing object is not a zero.

iiRead as decimals, never floats

Prices are parsed as decimals, so a tiny published price cannot round down to zero.

iiiClaims that are not proof

A scanner claim or a lagging mirror is marked with a dagger and left out by default.

ivThe intelligence floor

Artificial Analysis scores the model, not the route. A model below the floor is hidden.
