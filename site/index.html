How a route earns its entry

Three claims about price, one about quality. A route has to satisfy the first three before the fourth is even read.

iZero priced, dimension by dimension

The provider publishes a zero for every billable field: input, output, cache reads and writes, requests, images, audio and long context tiers. A missing pricing object is not a zero.

iiRead as decimals, never floats

Prices are parsed as decimals so a published one times ten to the minus nine hundred ninety ninth cannot round down to zero and pass as free.

iiiClaims that are not proof

A scanner that asserts a free tier, or a third party mirror that lags the provider, is marked with a dagger and left out unless you ask for it.

ivThe intelligence floor

Artificial Analysis scores the model, not the route. A model below the floor is hidden, and a model with no score at all is kept out of the register unless you ask for it.
